# The free AI tools I actually use in 2026 (and what just launched)

Meta description: A honest list of free AI tools that still work in 2026, plus the November launches that matter — no hype, just what I use.
Image suggestion: a clean desk/laptop photo with the tool logos, 1200x630.

I have a folder on my laptop called "ai-tools". It has bookmarks I never open and a few I use every single day. The difference between the two is simple: one group asks me to pay before I can finish a task, the other lets me finish it.

This is the second group.

## ChatGPT and Gemini are the default

I start almost everything in ChatGPT. The free tier is enough for explaining a concept, drafting a message, or unblocking me when I am stuck on a problem. Gemini is my second stop, especially when the answer needs to be grounded in something on the web, or when I am working inside Google Docs.

Neither is perfect. ChatGPT is better at talking me through a problem. Gemini is better when I need it to look something up. I switch between them without thinking about it.

## Perplexity for research

If I need to know something real, I go to Perplexity first. It shows its sources, which matters. Most AI answers sound confident and are wrong in small ways. Perplexity lets me check.

## Gemini Notebook for my own notes

Google renamed NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook this year. I upload my notes and ask questions about them. It is the tool I wish I had in school.

## Canva and CapCut for anything visual

Canva's free plan is still the easiest way to make a poster, a resume, or a social post. CapCut handles video. Both have paid tiers, but I have never needed them for personal work.

## GitHub Copilot for code

Students can get GitHub Copilot free through GitHub Education. If you are in school or college, claim it before you pay for anything else. It is the single best student discount in tech right now.

## What launched in November 2026

A lot. Most of it is paid, but some of it is not.

- Google launched Gemini 4 Hydrogen on November 12, and it is on the free API tier in Google AI Studio. That is the first Gemini 4 model free users can touch.
- Google made Tasks in Gemini free on November 17. Gemini can now call businesses, book appointments, and check out with Google Pay in 60+ apps.
- OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol on November 17. Both are paid, and free ChatGPT users do not get them.
- Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 on November 9, with a 1M token context window.
- Cognition launched Devin Bots, agents that manage teams of coding agents.
- Microsoft launched MAI Code 2 in GitHub Copilot.
- Alibaba released Qwen4-Max.

The pattern this month: agents that run longer tasks, and cheaper models for everyday work.

## What I would tell a beginner

Start with two tools, not ten. ChatGPT for thinking, Perplexity for checking. Add Gemini Notebook when you have notes to study. Add Canva when you need to make something look good. Ignore the rest until a real task forces you to learn it.

The tools are not the skill. Finishing things is the skill.
