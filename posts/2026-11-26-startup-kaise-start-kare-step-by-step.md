# Startup kaise start kare — step by step

Language: Hinglish (Roman Hindi mixed with English) | Date: 2026-11-26 | Provider: pollinations

Mainne pehle gaon ke ek chhote sa break ke baad Dhanbad ke college campus se milke startup ka idea socha. Idea, plan, execution – yeh sirf legal jargon nahi, balki ek realistic journey hai.

### 1. Problem ko identify karna

Mujhe pata chal gaya ki khud ka startup shuru karne se pehle sabse pehla kaam hai ek genuine problem find karna. Dhanbad ki mine streets mein chirpy coffee shop ka idea tha, par market research se pata chala ki log online deliveri pe jyada interest rakhte hain. Matlab, problem tumhare liye ek question hona chahiye, jiska jawab tum dekhne wale ho. Research ke liye Google Trends, Local forums, aur friends ka input liya – yeh steps agar bhi adhoore reh jaayein, toh yaad raho ki problem ko samajhna bina solution ke bhi kaafi valuable hota hai.

### 2. Solution ka skeleton banana

Jab problem clear ho jaye, toh uska solution ka skeleton banana theek hai. Maine ek simple app ka prototype socha jo custom coffee blends deliver kare. Wireframes banayi using Figma, aur ek quick prototype ki testing ki local users ke saath. Incidents se judne ke liye, kya usability issues aaye? Kya features ki kami mehsoos hui? Saath hi feedback loop banaya – har ek user ka input ek bar baar analyze kiya.

### 3. Business model aur revenue streams decide karna

Yeh ek common step hai jahan startups fail hote hain. Mere case mein, revenue streams do si – direct sale aur subscription model. Subscription ko ek minimum monthly fee rakhi aur direct sale ko low commission structure ke saath launch kiya. Profit margin kolculate ki aur break-even point nikal liya.

### 4. Minimum Viable Product (MVP) launch karna

MVP ke liye maine ek minimal feature set choose kiya: authentication, order placement, payment gateway integration. Use of Stripe was easy because it has a great API support for India. Development cycle mein Agile sprints adopt ki – har ek sprint end mein review aur demo. Uske baad ek small beta launch local users ke saath kiya aur actual use data collect kiya.

### 5. Legal paperwork aur compliance

Dhanbad ke local laws ke according, ek company register karna padta hai. I got a GST number, and did the necessary PAN registration. Maine online portal par “Shopify” ko rakha for small business compliance, because mainne initially thought of using a platform as a service. Yeh step sabke liye kaafi important hota hai, otherwise future scaling mein problems aayengi.

### 6. Funding aur bootstrapping

Startup ka funding sarkari grant ki tarah bhi se ho sakta hai. Maine Dhanbad ki local startup incubator se ek seed funding phir se come; uske alawa, friends and family ne micro contribution diya. Bootstrapping ke through mainne nirdisht expenses control ki – marketing ke liye aur beta test ke liye limited team ka hire kiya. Har ek month end mein kharcha evaluate kiya; profit re-invest kiya product improvement mein.

### 7. Marketing strategy: Low budget, high impact

Social media par content ka use kiya – short reels showing coffee blends and their aroma. Local community groups me bhi engagement ki. Next, I started a referral program where users got a discount for every friend they bring. Yeh strategy especially effective thi Dhanbad ke close-knit community ke liye.
